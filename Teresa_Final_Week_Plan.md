#### Part 1: Digit Reversal & Modular Arithmetic (10 min)
**Fix Problem 13 type:**

When you reverse two digits (like 37 → 73), the difference is always a multiple of 9:
- Original: $10a + b$
- Reversed: $10b + a$
- Difference: $(10b + a) - (10a + b) = 9b - 9a = 9(b-a)$

**Practice:**
- If you reverse 52 to 25, what's the difference? (27 = 9×3) ✓
- If you reverse 81 to 18, what's the difference? (63 = 9×7) ✓
- Which could be the difference: 45, 46, 47, 48, 49? → **45 only** (9×5)

**This fixes Problem 13!** ✓

#### Part 2: Exponent Equations (10 min)
**Fix Problem 15 type:**

**Solve step-by-step:**
1. $3^p + 81 = 90$ → $3^p = 9 = 3^2$ → $p = 2$ ✓
2. $2^r + 44 = 76$ → $2^r = 32 = 2^5$ → $r = 5$ ✓
3. $5^3 + 6^s = 1421$ → $125 + 6^s = 1421$ → $6^s = 1296 = 6^4$ → $s = 4$ ✓
4. Product: $2 \times 5 \times 4 = 40$ ✓

**Practice 2-3 similar problems:**

#### Part 3: Consecutive Integers (10 min)
**Fix Problem 17 type:**

**Formula for sum of consecutive integers starting at $n$:**
- Sum of $k$ consecutive integers = $kn + \frac{k(k-1)}{2}$

**For Problem 17:**
- 6 consecutive integers sum to 2013
- $6n + 15 = 2013$
- $6n = 1998$
- $n = 333$
- Largest = $333 + 5 = 338$ ✓

**Quick check:** Average = $2013 \div 6 = 335.5$, so integers are 333, 334, 335, 336, 337, 338 ✓

**Practice:**

- Sum of 5 consecutive integers = 100. What's the smallest?

**This fixes Day 10 Problem 17!** ✓

#### Part 4: Power of 2 Divisibility (15 min) **NEW FIX**
**Fix 2016 Problem 15 type:**

**Teach the method:**
Find the largest power of 2 that divides 13⁴ - 11⁴

**Step 1:** Use difference of squares repeatedly
- 13⁴ - 11⁴ = (13²)² - (11²)²
- = (13² + 11²)(13² - 11²)
- = (169 + 121)(169 - 121)
- = 290 × 48

**Step 2:** Factor out powers of 2
- 290 = 2 × 145 (only one factor of 2)
- 48 = 16 × 3 = 2⁴ × 3

**Step 3:** Count total powers of 2
- 290 × 48 = (2 × 145) × (2⁴ × 3)
- = 2⁵ × (145 × 3)
- **Answer: 2⁵ = 32** ✓

**Key pattern:** For difference of squares a² - b², always factor first, THEN find powers of 2.

**Practice:**
- What's the largest power of 2 dividing 10² - 6²? (64 = 2⁶)
- What's the largest power of 2 dividing 15² - 9²? (144 = 2⁴ × 9, so 2⁴ = 16)

**This fixes 2016 Problem 15!** ✓
